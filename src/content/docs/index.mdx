## What the control plane can and cannot see

Your agents, models and services run on your own machines. Agent-Fabric gives
them a private encrypted mesh (WireGuard) and a control plane that brokers keys,
device identity and policy.

The control plane sees **names, health and usage counts**. It does not see
prompts, model outputs or any traffic between your machines — those are
end-to-end encrypted, with a relay used only when two machines cannot reach each
other directly, and the relay carries ciphertext it cannot read.

If you only read one more page, read [How it works](/guides/how-it-works/).
